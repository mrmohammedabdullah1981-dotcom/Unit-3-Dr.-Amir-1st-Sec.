1. A Vision of the Future (Jules Verne)

In the late 1800s, French author Jules Verne wrote about the future and imagined technologies one hundred years ahead of his time:

  • From the Earth to the Moon: Characters traveled into space using a rocket.
  • 20,000 Leagues Under the Sea: Characters explored deep oceans in a submarine shaped like a whale.
  • Paris in the Twentieth Century: Skyscraper buildings made of glass and steel, gas-powered automobiles, high-speed bullet trains, and global communication networks.
Key Concept: Verne is celebrated as a visionary because many of the revolutionary inventions he imagined became tangible realities by the end of the 20th century.

2. Predictions Made in 1955: Reality vs Fiction

1955 Prediction Statement Status Today Curriculum Fact
"I seriously doubt that fast food restaurants will ever catch on." False Prediction Fast food chains became a global multi-billion dollar dining industry.
"$1,500 won't even buy a used car." True Prediction Because of inflation, modern used cars cost much more than $1,500.
"Sports players will make more money than the president." True Prediction Top athletes earn tens of millions yearly, far exceeding executive political salaries.
"Soon all kitchen appliances will be electric." True Prediction Refrigerators, dishwashers, and ovens are standard household electric appliances.

3. Grammar: Expressing Future & Making Predictions

A. Future with 'Will' vs 'Be Going To':

Structure Primary Usage Clear Example
Will + base verb Spontaneous decisions made at speech time, offers, promises, or predictions with uncertainty (maybe, probably). "We ran out of bread. I will go to the supermarket."
Be Going To + base verb Pre-planned events, intentions, or predictions based on current visual evidence. "This summer, I am going to take an art class."

B. Future Progressive (Continuous):

Rule: Use will + be + verb(-ing) OR am/is/are + going to be + verb(-ing) for an action that will be happening continuously at a specific point in the future.
Example: "By the year 2100, humans will be living (or are going to be living) on the moon."
